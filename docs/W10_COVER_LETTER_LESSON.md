# Week 10 — Human-First Cover Letter Lab

## Position in course
Jobfit STEP 10. Students write from verified Career Assets rather than asking AI to create a story.

## Learning outcomes
Students can:
1. identify the evaluation intent of a cover-letter question;
2. choose evidence that fits the question and Target JD;
3. use AI as an interviewer to elicit missing detail;
4. distinguish raw personal language, structured evidence and AI draft;
5. fact-check a draft before moving to final editing.

## Class flow
### Block A — Question intent
Students classify what the question is testing: motivation, job evidence, challenge, collaboration, values, growth, etc.

### Block B — Evidence selection
Use STEP 8 Career Assets to compare candidate experiences. Student makes the final choice.

### Block C — AI Experience Interview
AI asks one question at a time. Required checkpoints:
- student's direct action;
- why the action was chosen;
- result and evidence;
- job/company connection.

### Block D — Structure before prose
Build: core message → situation → action → result → job connection.

### Block E — Draft
Only after evidence is sufficient does AI produce a draft. Student checks all facts.

## AI use rule
AI is Interviewer + Structurer, not Ghostwriter. It cannot create missing evidence.

## Student outputs
- question intent
- selected Career Assets
- Raw Voice answers
- structured answer
- fact-checked cover-letter draft

## Bridge to Week 13
The draft is intentionally not treated as final. Human-First editing later restores the student's own language and checks consistency with interview answers.

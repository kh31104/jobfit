# Weeks 11–12 — Interview Strategy & Practice Lab

## Position in course
Jobfit STEP 11. Students verbally defend the same evidence already used in resume and cover letter.

## Week 11 focus — Core interview types
- 1-minute introduction
- motivation / company-choice criteria
- personality and work-style questions
- experience questions
- job-related questions

Teaching formula for experience answers:
Conclusion → Situation → Action → Result → Job connection.

Students build a personal Question Bank from their own resume, cover letter and Career Assets.

## Week 12 focus — Advanced / follow-up practice
- situational questions
- collaboration/conflict
- failure and feedback
- pressure/follow-up questions
- PT/presentation logic where relevant
- repeated evidence verification

## AI practice rule
AI plays interviewer, not answer writer.
- one question at a time;
- ask follow-ups about direct action, reasoning and evidence;
- flag vague answers;
- do not fill missing facts;
- give short feedback on clarity, evidence and job connection.

## Student outputs
- personal Interview Question Bank
- evidence-linked answer outlines
- follow-up questions
- practice feedback log
- next-practice improvement point

## Instructor observation focus
- Does the student answer the question first?
- Can they distinguish team action from personal action?
- Can they explain why they acted that way?
- Can they defend numbers/results?
- Does the spoken answer match the written application?

## Bridge to Week 13
Week 13 performs a full consistency check: Resume ↔ Cover Letter ↔ Interview ↔ Career Asset.

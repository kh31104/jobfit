# Week 13 — Human-First Writing & Consistency Check

## Position in course
Jobfit STEP 12. This is not an AI-detector evasion class. It is an authorship, fact and consistency class.

## Learning outcomes
Students can:
1. distinguish useful AI structure from generic/unnatural wording;
2. preserve facts while editing tone and sentence structure;
3. use semester-long Raw Voice as a reference;
4. classify text as KEEP / HUMANIZE / DELETE;
5. verify consistency across resume, cover letter and interview.

## Core message
Humanize는 나쁜 내용을 좋은 내용으로 고치는 단계가 아니다. 좋은 내용을 사람의 언어로 되돌리는 단계다.

## Class flow
### Block A — Raw Voice review
Students read expressions they used before AI editing in STEP 2.

### Block B — KEEP / HUMANIZE / DELETE
- KEEP: concrete, factual, sounds plausible in the student's own speech/writing.
- HUMANIZE: necessary content but overly abstract, translated or formulaic.
- DELETE: generic filler with little information.

### Block C — Minimal-change editing
Keep actions, numbers, roles and results locked. Remove unsupported claims and unnecessary decorative modifiers.

### Block D — Speak it
Student reads or explains the final paragraph aloud. If the written answer cannot be explained naturally, revisit wording or evidence.

### Block E — Cross-document consistency
Check:
- role and date consistency;
- numerical consistency;
- same experience facts across documents;
- ability to explain every written claim orally.

## AI use rule
Do not ask AI to “make this undetectable.” Ask it to identify generic phrasing, unsupported claims, translation-like phrasing and places where Raw Voice differs. Final choice remains the student's.

## Student outputs
- Human-First final cover-letter version
- Fact Lock confirmation
- Resume ↔ Cover Letter ↔ Interview consistency check
- final revision notes

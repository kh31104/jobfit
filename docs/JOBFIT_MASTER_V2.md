# Jobfit v2 MASTER

## Goal
Jobfit is one integrated AI Career & Job Application platform used across full-roadmap classes, selective modules, regular university courses, and external workshops.

## Core learning flow
SELF → EXPERIENCE → COMPETENCY → JOB → INDUSTRY/COMPANY → JD → FIT → APPLICATION → INTERVIEW → PORTFOLIO

## Modes
1. Full Career Roadmap: STEP 0→13 sequentially.
2. Selective Career Tools: student uses needed modules only.
3. Course configuration is not a separate mode. A course code can lock the full roadmap, set the current week, specify S or L interest test, and enable research consent/measurement slots.

## STEP 0–13
- STEP 0 Career Start: anonymous code, profile, course code, research consent, career baseline, AI baseline.
- STEP 1 Career DNA: Work24 S or L (not both), Work24 work values, VIA TOP5 for learning use, student agreement/disagreement reflection.
- STEP 2 Experience & Competency: raw experience → AI interview → action/result/evidence/competency.
- STEP 3 Job Explorer: broad job exploration and shortlist.
- STEP 4 Job Deep Dive: Task/KSA/KPI/tools/work environment/required experience.
- STEP 5 Industry & Company Explorer: industry structure, market, customer, company, issues, role connection.
- STEP 6 Career Fit Map: ME × JOB × INDUSTRY × COMPANY.
- STEP 7 JD Analyzer: real posting → Task/KSA/competency signals.
- STEP 8 Career Asset DB: reusable evidence bank for applications.
- STEP 9 Resume Lab: resume and experience descriptions.
- STEP 10 Cover Letter Lab: question intent → evidence selection → AI interview → draft.
- STEP 11 Interview Lab: evidence-based answers, follow-ups, PT/discussion/situational practice.
- STEP 12 Human-First Check: KEEP/HUMANIZE/DELETE, fact check, resume-cover letter-interview consistency.
- STEP 13 AI Job Portfolio: assemble actual job-ready application package.

## AI use rule
Jobfit stores learner data, evidence, selected outputs, and portfolio artifacts. External AI (ChatGPT/Gemini/Claude etc.) is used for prompt practice, interviewing, analysis, critique, and drafting. Jobfit should provide prompt copy, result paste, student revision, and final selection rather than automate every AI action.

## Research architecture
Learning data and research data must be separated.

### Learner data
Experience, raw voice, competency evidence, company/JD analysis, resume, cover letter, interview artifacts, portfolio.

### Research Core (consent only)
- anonymous participant code
- cohort/program/institution metadata
- age, gender, grade, major/major group, graduation status
- career/employment baseline
- AI-use baseline
- Work24 S OR L result (never require both)
- Work24 work-values result
- Work24 college career-readiness result when used
- PRE/POST assessment slots
- K-CAAS-SF only after permission is confirmed
- Korean Strength Use & Deficit Correction scale only after permission is confirmed

VIA TOP5 is primarily a teaching tool and is not a required research variable.

## Data principles
- Preserve raw student response separately from AI-structured response.
- Store assessment name, version, date, and full subscale/item values where permission and collection method allow.
- Do not store names/student numbers in the research table.
- Research opt-out must not block learning functionality.
- Static GitHub Pages cannot by itself provide a secure central research database; backend/database integration is a separate implementation layer.

## Course configuration examples
- regular course: full roadmap locked, anonymous code enabled, research consent available, instructor-specified S/L assessment.
- external workshop: full or selective mode, optional anonymous code, optional research consent.
- public user: full or selective mode, no course restrictions.

## Final portfolio
Career DNA → Target Job → Industry Analysis → Company Analysis → Target JD → Career Assets → FIT → Resume → Experience Description → Cover Letter → Interview Evidence → Career GAP → 30/90-day Action Plan.

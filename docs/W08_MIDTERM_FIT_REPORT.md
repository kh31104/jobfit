# Week 8 — Midterm: ME × JOB × INDUSTRY × COMPANY FIT Report

## Position in course
Jobfit STEP 6. This is the midpoint decision checkpoint, not a final career decision.

## Learning outcomes
Students can:
1. compare multiple job–industry–company combinations with explicit evidence;
2. distinguish a self-rating decision aid from a validated psychological score;
3. state why one direction currently deserves deeper exploration;
4. identify uncertainties and gaps that must be checked through real job postings;
5. produce a midterm Career FIT Report from accumulated Jobfit data.

## Assessment principle
The midterm does not reward choosing a prestigious company or producing a high FIT number. It evaluates the quality of evidence, comparison logic and reflection.

## Required evidence before assessment
- Career Start baseline;
- Career DNA;
- Experience & Competency evidence;
- Target Job 1–3;
- at least one Job Deep Dive;
- Industry Pool and Target Industry;
- Company Pool and Target Company.

## FIT comparison criteria
Students rate each combination on a 1–5 self-assessment scale:
- role interest;
- work-value fit;
- current experience evidence;
- industry appeal;
- company appeal;
- confidence in the information gathered.

The arithmetic mean is displayed only as a comparison indicator. It is explicitly labeled as a decision-support metric, not a psychometric fit score.

## Required written evidence
Every compared combination must include:
- FIT reasons;
- risk / uncertainty / GAP.

## Midterm output
ME × JOB × INDUSTRY × COMPANY FIT Report:
1. current Career Evidence;
2. compared pathways;
3. current priority pathway;
4. reasons and evidence;
5. current GAP and uncertainty;
6. midpoint reflection.

## Instructor grading focus
Suggested emphasis:
- 30% evidence quality and source use;
- 25% quality of job/industry/company comparison;
- 20% connection between self evidence and external job information;
- 15% realistic GAP/uncertainty recognition;
- 10% reflection and clarity.

Do not grade the numeric comparison indicator itself.

## Bridge to Week 9
The selected pathway is a working hypothesis. Week 9 tests it against a real recruitment posting. Students may revise the target if the JD evidence does not support the earlier assumption.
